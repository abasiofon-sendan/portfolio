## Problem and constraints

A fintech partner relied on webhooks to move money events into their accounting system. When a delivery failed, the event was lost. Support tickets followed. The constraints: no downtime during migration, and the retry logic had to survive process restarts.

## Key decisions

- Store every delivery attempt in SQLite instead of in-memory, so no attempt is lost on restart.
- Use an outbox pattern: events land in a queue table before any delivery attempt.
- Make retries exponential with jitter, capped at 12 attempts over 24 hours.

## The hard part

Ordering. Replaying events out of order broke the accounting totals. The fix was a per-source sequence number with a simple conflict check before insert.

## Results and lessons

Failed deliveries dropped from 6% to under 0.5% in the first month. The outbox pattern is now the default for every new integration I build.
