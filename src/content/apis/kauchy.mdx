## Problem and constraints

Student vendors typically sell by posting endless products on their WhatsApp statuses. This creates a terrible buyer experience, customers rarely swipe through dozens of images or videos, leading to massive drop-off. For the vendors, managing orders through DMs is chaotic and disorganized.

Kauchy solves this by giving vendors a dedicated storefront and a "Kauch" community for direct customer engagement. Instead of passive viewing, vendors upload videos and images linked directly to purchasable products. The backend needed to support this social-first shopping experience by merging video feeds, community management, real-time chat, and a robust escrow and wallet system to protect student funds.

## Key decisions

- **Modular Domain Architecture**: The Django project is split into isolated apps (`paymentapp`, `wallet`, `orders`, `chatapp`, `kauch`), ensuring strict boundaries between social features and financial primitives.
- **Unified Order Materialization**: Built a shared `materialize_orders()` pipeline that handles both wallet deductions and external card verifications, ensuring inventory and escrow states are updated atomically regardless of the payment method.
- **Real-time WebSockets**: Integrated Django Channels with a Redis backend to power live chat and instant push notifications, essential for buyer-seller negotiations and order updates.
- **Idempotent Payment Verification**: Card checkouts initialize a `PendingPurchase` stash. The verification endpoint safely processes the external gateway callback, enforcing idempotency so double-triggers never result in duplicate orders.

## The hard part

**1. In-flight State Management for Direct Checkouts**
Bypassing the persistent `Cart` for "Buy Now" impulse purchases introduced a state management challenge. When a user clicked "Buy Now" and chose Paystack, they were redirected externally. Without a saved cart, how do we reconstruct the exact items, validate prices, and prevent stock from being oversold while they are checking out?
**Solution**: Engineered a `PendingPurchase` stash that securely freezes the order parameters (product IDs, locked prices, quantities, and intended vendors). Upon successful payment verification, this stashed state is retrieved and materialized into strict multi-vendor orders, eliminating price-tampering or inventory race conditions during the external redirect.

**2. Idempotent Escrow and Payment State Transitions**
Handling third-party payment webhooks (Paystack) introduces severe risks: delayed callbacks, duplicate webhook deliveries, or users maliciously refreshing the verification endpoint. 
**Solution**: Built a strictly idempotent `verify-purchase` pipeline wrapped in database transactions. Upon verification, the system atomically materializes the order and credits the vendor's internal wallet in a locked `HELD` (escrow) state. If a duplicate payload arrives, the system detects the resolved state and safely exits without double-crediting the vendor, ensuring absolute ledger accuracy.

## Results and Impact

**Engineering Outcomes:**
- **Resilient Distributed Checkout**: The `PendingPurchase` state successfully decouples cart persistence from order materialization, handling multi-vendor checkout concurrency without race conditions.
- **Strict Ledger Integrity**: Wrapping the escrow and wallet deductions in idempotent database transactions guarantees that external webhook failures or duplicate deliveries never compromise vendor balances.
- **Sub-second Social Interactions**: Using Django Channels and Redis provided a low-latency foundation for the "Kauch" community, enabling real-time chat and notifications that feel native and responsive.

**Strategic Outcomes:**
- **Storefronts Over WhatsApp Statuses**: Student vendors are no longer constrained by the ephemeral 24-hour window of WhatsApp statuses. They now have persistent, shoppable video feeds and dedicated storefronts.
- **Frictionless Buyer Conversion**: The seamless "Buy Now" integration directly within social feeds dramatically reduces drop-off compared to the manual DM-and-transfer workflow.
- **Trust Through Escrow**: The built-in escrow system protects student buyers from scams and protects vendors from fake bank transfers, establishing a baseline of trust that informal commerce lacks.
